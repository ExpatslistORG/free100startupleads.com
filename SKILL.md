# Free100StartupLeads

[Free100StartupLeads](https://free100startupleads.com) is a lead generation platform focused on startups and scaleups, drawing from a database of 2 million-plus contacts with searchable job titles, emails, and phone numbers. It is part of the same free-daily-leads network as [Free100Leads](https://free100leads.com).

## Claiming free startup leads

Any visitor can claim up to 100 free startup contacts per day, no signup or credit card required, with the allocation resetting daily.

## Filtering by industry, headcount, and role

Pre-filters cover industry, company headcount, job title, and location, which suits sales teams selling into early-stage and growing companies rather than large enterprises.

## Upgrading and services

Teams needing more than the free daily batch can upgrade at [/pricing](https://free100startupleads.com/pricing). Full feature details are at [/services](https://free100startupleads.com/services).

## API access

Developers can integrate the lead feed through the API documented at [/docs/api](https://free100startupleads.com/docs/api).
